\section{Discussion}
\paragraph{Verdict on the hypothesis.}
We expected the low-latency loss to come mainly from frequency resolution
rather than from the missing future. The decomposition supports half of
that. The future is essentially worthless to these models: non-causal
twins and matched-hop lookahead change SI-SDRi by a few hundredths of a dB.
But the window is not the main cost either. The oracle says a 4\,ms window
costs \GapResolutionFourms\,dB, while learning costs
\LongGapLearningFourms\,dB even after 12k steps. The 1\,dB gap criterion is
met, but only because short hops train faster per step, so we treat it as
uninformative rather than as confirmation. The cue criterion fails on
timing: no model, and not even the oracle mask, keeps $|\Delta$ITD$|$
under 20\,$\mu$s, while $|\Delta$ILD$|$ is comfortably under 1\,dB. The
window-versus-lookahead criterion does not falsify the hypothesis:
lookahead buys nothing at a matched hop.

\paragraph{What this means for a hearing device.}
Within a 5\,ms budget, the cheapest way to improve quality is not latency
but capacity, data, and training. Strict causality removes almost nothing
that a small model could use. A 4\,ms AsymDF already streams at RTF
\RtfstftasymFourms\ on one CPU thread. The open problem is binaural timing:
a mask that leaves even a little noise in the low band moves the perceived
location. That argues for cue-preserving structure (common gains below
1.5\,kHz) or an ITD-specific loss, not for more lookahead.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The models are small (\stftasymFourmsearParams k parameters for
AsymDF) and far from the oracle, so whether the future becomes useful once
the learning gap closes is untested. The GPU configuration (1--2M
parameters, 40k steps, $\approx$100\,h of speech) is sized to answer that.
\item Training at equal steps confounds latency with supervision density.
A frame-matched budget would be cleaner.
\item The target keeps early reflections, so SI-SDRi is not comparable to
dry-target benchmarks.
\item There is one seed per cell.
\item PESQ was unavailable on the build machine.
\item All mixtures are simulated, although with real speech, real noise and
measured heads.
\end{itemize}
