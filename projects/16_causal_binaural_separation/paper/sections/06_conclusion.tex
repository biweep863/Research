\section{Conclusion}
For causal binaural enhancement inside a hearing-aid budget, we split the
cost of low latency into three parts:
\begin{itemize}
\item the window, about 1\,dB from 32\,ms to 1\,ms even for an ideal mask,
and partly recoverable with asymmetric windows;
\item the future, $\approx$0\,dB for the networks we trained;
\item learning, several dB, by far the largest.
\end{itemize}
Level cues survive and timing cues do not. A strictly causal 4\,ms model
runs comfortably in real time on one CPU thread. The practical message is
that ``hearing the future'' is not what separates today's small causal
models from good quality, and that the binaural ITD, not the SI-SDR, is the
harder target. Code, configurations and the exact commands are in the
project repository; \texttt{make all} reproduces every number in this
paper.
