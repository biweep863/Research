# 01 · Dataset
5 templates × 2 surface forms × 64 items = 640 prompts (local).
Templates: modus_ponens, modus_tollens, syllogism_barbara, and_chain, or_chain.
Surfaces:
- English: natural sentences with "Therefore … This argument is __".
- Conlang: hand-designed predicate calculus with invented predicate words
  (`glip`, `blarn`, …), connectives (`tav` = AND, `lus` = OR, `nyx` = NOT,
  `drev` = →, `qol` = ∴, `vir` = YES, `kal` = NO).

The labels are perfectly balanced (50% valid, 50% invalid) at every
template-surface intersection.
