\section{Experiments}

\paragraph{Models.} GPT-2 small (124M parameters, 12 blocks, 12 heads/block,
local pilot) and Pythia-2.8B (32 blocks, 32 heads/block, GPU-scale run).
The ablation hook required a GPT-NeoX/Pythia branch (Pythia's attention
output projection lives at a different module path than GPT-2's),
verified against the tiny \texttt{pythia-70m} before the full sweep.

\paragraph{Data.} Local pilot: $5$ templates $\times2$ surfaces $\times64$
items $=640$ prompts. GPU-scale: $10$ templates (adding four categorical-
syllogism moods and XOR/double-negation chains, absent from the local
generator and implemented for this run) $\times2$ surfaces $\times256$
items $=5120$ prompts. Generation is fully deterministic given the seed.

\paragraph{Probes.} 5-fold stratified cross-validated logistic regression
($C=1.0$, $\ell_2$-regularised). One probe per (layer, surface).

\paragraph{Ablation.} All attention heads are ablated one at a time: $144$
for GPT-2 ($12\times12$), $1024$ for Pythia-2.8B ($32\times32$), scored on
(a) logic accuracy on a held-out subset of $80$ prompts and (b) LM
cross-entropy on a fixed neutral snippet. The GPT-2 sweep completes in
about 2.5 minutes on CPU; the Pythia-2.8B sweep in about 37 minutes on a
12GB GPU ($\approx2.2$s/head).

\paragraph{Baseline.} With no ablation, GPT-2 achieves $\gptTwoBaselineAcc$
next-token accuracy on the validity-prediction task; Pythia-2.8B achieves
$\pythiaTwoEightbBaselineAcc$ -- \emph{below} chance on the 10-template
mix, i.e.\ the raw zero-shot preference between `` valid'' and `` invalid''
is miscalibrated in direction on average, not merely noisy. Both baselines
are near or below chance, so probes carry the load of detecting internal
structure the model does not straightforwardly act on.
