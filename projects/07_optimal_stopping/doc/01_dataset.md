# 01 · Data (synthetic environments)

There is no external dataset; all "data" are episodes generated by the
environments in `src/envs/`. This keeps the project fully reproducible and
network-free.

## Secretary environment (`envs/secretary.py`)
Each episode is a uniform random permutation of `n` distinct candidates
(value 0 denotes the best). Only relative ranks are exposed. A vectorised
Monte-Carlo routine scores threshold policies over batches of permutations;
`SecretaryEpisode` exposes the sequence of running records for RL agents.

## Parking environment (`envs/parking.py`)
A street is the distance sequence `[k, k-1, ..., 1, 0, 1, ..., k]` (length
`2k+1`), approaching a destination at distance 0 and passing it. Each spot is
free independently with probability `p` (Bernoulli). `ParkingEpisode` draws one
free/occupied realization and exposes the free spots in order.

## Configurations
- `local_small.yaml`: `n ∈ {10,100,1000}` (agents on `{10,50,100}`),
  `p ∈ {0.3,0.5,0.7,0.9}`, street half-length `k=8`, `5×10^4` MC episodes.
- `gpu_full.yaml`: wider sweeps, `k=20`, more occupancies and costs, `5×10^5`
  MC episodes, 10 seeds. CPU-only — the "GPU" config is just a larger budget.
