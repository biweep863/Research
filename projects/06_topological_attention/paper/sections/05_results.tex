\paragraph{Power analysis.} With observed standard deviations
($\approx 2.1$ on $H_0$ total persistence) and observed mean
difference ($\approx 0.07$), a two-sample $t$-test at
$\alpha=0.05$, $\beta=0.2$ needs $N\approx 200$ per condition. The
\texttt{gpu\_full.yaml} configuration is sized accordingly, with the
intent of running on TruthfulQA pairs.

\paragraph{Headline.} On the pilot, the minimum per-layer
$p$-value is $\gptTwoMinPval$ at layer $\gptTwoMinPvalLayer$ on
$H_0$. No layer reaches significance ($\gptTwoNumSigLayers$ of
$\gptTwoNumLayers$). The pipeline is validated and ready to scale.
