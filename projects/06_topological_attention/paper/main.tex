\begin{abstract}
\noindent We test the hypothesis that hallucinated continuations of factual
prompts shift the persistent-homology summary statistics of the attention
graph relative to truthful continuations. On GPT-2 across $\gptTwoNumPairs$
hand-curated truthful/hallucinated pairs (e.g.\ ``The capital of France
is Paris'' vs.\ ``Berlin''), we compute the Vietoris--Rips persistent
homology of the per-layer attention-distance matrix and report the total
persistence in $H_0$ and $H_1$. The local pilot, by design, is
underpowered: with only $\gptTwoNumPairs$ pairs, no layer reaches
$p<0.05$ (minimum $p=\gptTwoMinPval$ at layer
$\gptTwoMinPvalLayer$, on $H_0$). The mean $H_0$ totals are
$\gptTwoTruthHZeroMean$ (truthful) vs.\ $\gptTwoHallHZeroMean$
(hallucinated), giving an effect-size estimate that allows us to
power-plan the larger run.
\end{abstract}
